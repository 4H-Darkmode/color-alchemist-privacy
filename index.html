Color Alchemist

Privacy Policy — Last updated: March 29, 2026

Color Alchemist ("the App") is a free-to-play puzzle game developed by Kakhi Kapanadze ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or process any personal data. The App does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to function. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads (via mediation) to display rewarded advertisements. These services may collect and use certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google and Unity in accordance with their respective Privacy Policies:
Google Privacy Policy
Unity Ads Privacy Policy

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings or via the in-app Privacy Settings. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization". In the App, you can change your consent preferences at any time via the "Privacy Settings" button in the settings menu.

3. How Ads Are Used

The App shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to revive after a game over). No ads are shown without your explicit action. We do not serve banner ads, interstitial ads, or any ads that appear without your consent.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, we use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to request your permission to process data for advertising purposes. You have the right to withdraw or change your consent at any time using the "Privacy Settings" option within the App's settings menu.

5. Data Storage

The App does not store any data on external servers. Game progress and user preferences (such as grid size and speed settings) are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, the risk of a data breach from our end is minimal. However, third-party services like AdMob and Unity Ads handle data according to their own security practices.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. For data collected by third-party ad services, please refer to Google's data management tools: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy
