Color Alchemist

Privacy Policy — Last updated: March 8, 2026

Color Alchemist ("the App") is a free-to-play puzzle game developed by Kakhi Kapanadze ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or process any personal data. The App does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to function. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or the equivalent option on your device).

3. How Ads Are Used

The App shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to revive after a game over). No ads are shown without your explicit action. We do not serve banner ads, interstitial ads, or any ads that appear without your consent.

4. Data Storage

The App does not store any data on external servers. All game progress (scores, settings) exists only in the current session and is not persisted between app launches. No user data is transmitted to our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, the risk of a data breach from our end is minimal. However, third-party services like AdMob handle data according to their own security practices.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. For data collected by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's data management tools at: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
